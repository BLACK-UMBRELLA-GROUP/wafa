------------------------------------------------------------
CONTRACT GENERATION FEE
------------------------------------------------------------

The platform charges a service fee for generating,
managing and securing Islamic contracts.

The fee is NOT related to Riba (interest) or lending
money. It is a charge for the AI-powered contract
generation and lifecycle management service.

------------------------------------------------------------
PRICING MODEL
------------------------------------------------------------

Contract Fee = 0.5% of Contract Value
Minimum Fee: £1.99
Maximum Fee: TBC (to be reviewed after launch)

------------------------------------------------------------
PAYMENT FLOW
------------------------------------------------------------

1. AI generates the draft contract.
2. Users review and edit the contract as many times
   as required (free during draft stage).
3. When both parties approve the final version, the
   contract fee is displayed.
4. Payment is completed.
5. Digital signatures are unlocked.
6. Once signed, the contract becomes active and enters
   lifecycle management.
