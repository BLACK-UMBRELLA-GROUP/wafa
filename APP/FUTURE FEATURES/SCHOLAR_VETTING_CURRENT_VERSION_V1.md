# SCHOLAR VETTING — CURRENT VERSION V1

| Field | Value |

| Status | CURRENT_VERSION — process decided 10 Nov 2026; nothing is built (the Scholar Marketplace is a later feature) |
| Founder's words | "Through partner organisations (Recommended)" · "Yes, every year (Recommended)" |
| ROADMAP row | "Define scholar vetting and approval" (3 h) - finished by this decision |
| Not a ruling | WAFA approving a scholar means WAFA checked who they are and who vouches for them. It does not certify their rulings, and the app must never present a scholar's answer as WAFA's ruling |

## 1. Who can be approved

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| 1 | Only scholars **put forward by a partner organisation** (a mosque, college or institution with a written partnership with WAFA). No open applications, no self-sign-up |
| 2 | The partner names the scholar and what they may do on WAFA: review agreements, explain clauses, answer contract questions, act as a witness |
| 3 | The scholar has a **Verified** badge (LOCK 3: photo ID and selfie) |
| 4 | Two references: one from the partner, one independent (another scholar or institution) |

## 2. Approval steps

| Step | Who | What |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Partner | Sends the nomination: name, role, what they may do, reference 1 |
| 2 | Scholar | Accepts, becomes Verified, gives reference 2, agrees to WAFA's scholar terms (written later) |
| 3 | WAFA | Checks the identity matches the nomination and both references reply |
| 4 | WAFA | Approves, refuses with a reason, or asks for more. The decision and date are recorded |
| 5 | App | Shows "Approved scholar · {partner name}" next to their name |

## 3. Staying approved

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Yearly check | Each year the partner confirms the scholar is still with them and in good standing; ID still valid |
| Complaints | Any member can report a concern from an agreement; WAFA reviews straight away and can pause the scholar while it looks |
| Partner ends | If the partnership ends or the partner withdraws the nomination, approval pauses until another partner puts them forward |
| Record | Every approval, check, pause and removal is kept (nothing is deleted) |
