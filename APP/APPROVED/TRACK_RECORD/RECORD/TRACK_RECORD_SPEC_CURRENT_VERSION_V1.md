## 1. Rules (fairness review)

| # | Rule | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Facts only, never a number, rank or level** | A single number is easy to game and can shame people |
| 2 | **Private by default.** The person chooses to share it with the people on their agreements, and can stop sharing at any time | People decide what others see about them |
| 3 | **Behaviour only.** Amounts, wealth, income and the type of agreement are never used or shown | Approved Future Features: behaviour, not wealth |
| 4 | An extension both sides agreed is **never counted as late** | Giving someone in difficulty more time is not a failing |
| 5 | New members show their facts like anyone else; nothing marks them down for being new | |
| 6 | Disputes are not shown until the dispute flow is designed | Disputes deferred (LOCK 1) |
| 7 | Demo: facts are fixed fictional examples per person (§4); real counting rules are built with a backend | |
