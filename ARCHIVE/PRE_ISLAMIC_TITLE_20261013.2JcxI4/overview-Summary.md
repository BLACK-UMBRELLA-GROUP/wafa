------------------------------------------------------------
PROJECT VISION
------------------------------------------------------------

Build the world's leading AI-powered Islamic contract
platform that enables Muslims to create, manage, sign,
fulfil and preserve agreements according to Islamic
principles.

The platform is built around:
  • Amanah (Trust)
  • Justice
  • Transparency
  • Accountability
  • Fulfilling agreements

The application NEVER generates contracts containing
Riba (interest). AI actively protects users by identifying
prohibited clauses and suggesting halal alternatives.

------------------------------------------------------------
USER JOURNEY
------------------------------------------------------------

 1. Create Contract
 2. AI asks conversational questions
 3. AI generates an Islamic-compliant contract
 4. Users review
 5. Unlimited edits
 6. AI regenerates
 7. Both parties approve
 8. Contract fee displayed
 9. Payment completed
10. Digital signatures collected
11. Witnesses sign
12. Contract becomes locked
13. Timeline begins
14. Smart reminders continue until completion
