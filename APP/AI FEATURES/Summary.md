Feature: AI Features

============================================================
SUMMARY
============================================================

------------------------------------------------------------
AI CONTRACT REVIEWER
------------------------------------------------------------

Users can upload an existing contract.
AI provides:
  • Plain English explanation
  • Clause-by-clause review
  • Missing clauses
  • Potential risks
  • Observations on alignment with the community's values
  • Suggested improvements

------------------------------------------------------------
AI VOICE CONTRACT CREATION
------------------------------------------------------------

Users simply speak naturally.
Example: "I'm lending Ahmed £500 until December."
AI automatically creates the contract.

------------------------------------------------------------
AI RISK CHECKER
------------------------------------------------------------

Automatically checks for:
  • Missing witnesses
  • Missing repayment dates
  • Missing signatures
  • Ambiguous wording
  • Missing contract values
  • Missing collateral where applicable
  • Missing important clauses

Displays warnings before signing.

------------------------------------------------------------
AI CONTRACT SUMMARY
------------------------------------------------------------

Generates a one-page summary of every contract.

Includes:
  • Contract Type
  • Parties
  • Amount
  • Repayment Date
  • Interest-Free status (Qard Hasan)
  • Witnesses (Shahid)
  • Collateral (Rahn)
  • Current Status

Designed so users can understand a contract within seconds.

------------------------------------------------------------
AI SMART SEARCH
------------------------------------------------------------

Natural language searching.
Examples:
  • Show unpaid loans.
  • Show contracts with Ahmed.
  • Show contracts ending next month.
  • Show completed contracts this year.

------------------------------------------------------------
AI CLAUSE EXPLAINER
------------------------------------------------------------

Tap any clause.
AI explains:
  • Meaning
  • Purpose
  • Plain English version
  • The community's religious considerations
  • Possible risks

------------------------------------------------------------
AI CLAUSE SUGGESTIONS
------------------------------------------------------------

AI suggests improvements.
Examples:
  • Add witnesses
  • Add repayment date
  • Clarify payment wording
  • Clarify collateral
  • Improve wording

Users choose whether to accept AI suggestions.

------------------------------------------------------------
AI REMINDER ASSISTANT
------------------------------------------------------------

Instead of generic reminders, AI creates respectful
personalised messages.

Example:
  "As-salamu alaykum.
  This is a friendly reminder that your agreed
  repayment date is approaching."

------------------------------------------------------------
AI DOCUMENT SCANNER
------------------------------------------------------------

Take a photo of an existing paper contract.
AI extracts:
  • Names
  • Dates
  • Amounts
  • Signatures
  • Important clauses

Creates a digital contract record from the scan.

------------------------------------------------------------
AI CONTRACT HISTORY
------------------------------------------------------------

Every version permanently stored.

  Version 1
  ↓
  Version 2
  ↓
  Version 3

Nothing is overwritten. Every change is permanently
recorded.

------------------------------------------------------------
ISLAMIC FINANCE TRACKER
------------------------------------------------------------

Users can monitor:
  • Money Lent
  • Money Owed
  • Outstanding Repayments
  • Upcoming Repayments
  • Historical Contracts
  • Completed Contracts

Future: dashboards with charts and insights.

------------------------------------------------------------
FUTURE AI FEATURES
------------------------------------------------------------

LIVE MARKET VALUATION
  For Rahn (Collateral), AI may estimate current market
  values where reliable data exists.
  Examples: Gold / Silver / Vehicles / Property (estimated)
  Contract distinguishes between:
  • Estimated Market Value
  • Agreed Value

TRANSLATION VERIFICATION
  AI compares every translated version to ensure
  consistency across languages.

CONTRACT LIFECYCLE INTELLIGENCE
  AI monitors the entire lifecycle of the agreement.
  Examples:
  • Repayment approaching
  • Long periods of inactivity
  • Outstanding collateral
  • Missing confirmations
  • Repayment completed but collateral not yet returned
  AI recommends appropriate next actions.

INTELLIGENT FINANCIAL GUIDANCE
  Private AI assistant provides educational guidance
  based on optional user responses.
  Examples:
  • Cash flow considerations
  • Repayment planning
  • General zakat considerations
  This guidance always remains private.
