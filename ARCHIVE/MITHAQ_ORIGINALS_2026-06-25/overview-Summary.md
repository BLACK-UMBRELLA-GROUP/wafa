PROJECT
MITHAQ (ميثاق)
Pronunciation: Mee-thaak

============================================================
OVERVIEW
============================================================

Meaning:
  • Covenant
  • Solemn Agreement
  • Binding Pledge

The name reflects the platform's purpose: helping Muslims
create and honour agreements according to Islamic principles.

Status:
  Approved as the current working name.

Future:
  • Domain availability check
  • Trademark review
  • App Store availability

------------------------------------------------------------
PROJECT VISION
------------------------------------------------------------

Build the world's leading AI-powered Islamic contract
platform that enables Muslims to create, manage, sign,
fulfil and preserve agreements according to Islamic
principles.

The platform is built around:
  • Amanah (Trust)
  • Justice
  • Transparency
  • Accountability
  • Fulfilling agreements

The application NEVER generates contracts containing
Riba (interest). AI actively protects users by identifying
prohibited clauses and suggesting halal alternatives.

------------------------------------------------------------
CORE PRINCIPLES

• Modern UI

------------------------------------------------------------
DESIGN PHILOSOPHY
------------------------------------------------------------

Mithaq is not designed to feel like legal software.

It should feel:
  • Premium
  • Minimal
  • Modern
  • Trustworthy
  • Calm
  • Easy to use

Design inspiration:
  • Apple Wallet
  • Stripe
  • Notion

Simple interface. No clutter.

------------------------------------------------------------
USER JOURNEY
------------------------------------------------------------

 1. Create Contract
 2. AI asks conversational questions
 3. AI generates an Islamic-compliant contract
 4. Users review
 5. Unlimited edits
 6. AI regenerates
 7. Both parties approve
 8. Contract fee displayed
 9. Payment completed
10. Digital signatures collected
11. Witnesses sign
12. Contract becomes locked
13. Timeline begins
14. Smart reminders continue until completion
